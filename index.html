Deckwell

The boards, joists, screws and gaps to buy and lay a timber deck. Put in the deck size and your board, and it sizes the frame and the shopping list.

The deck

Width is across the boards; length is the way the boards run.

The board

Softwood deck boards are usually 144 mm wide in 3.6 m lengths. The gap drains the deck and lets the wood move (TDCA: 5–8 mm).

How it's laid

A diagonal lay crosses the joists at an angle, so the joists come closer (300 mm) and more board is cut to waste.

Railing (optional)

Give the length of open edge you'll rail and how far the deck sits above the ground. Over 600 mm and a guard is required; Deckwell spaces the spindles so a 100 mm sphere can't pass (gaps 99 mm max, Approved Document K).

Planning figures to the TDCA (Timber Decking & Cladding Association) builder's guidance. Check joist spans against C16 timber span tables and your local rules before you buy. Runs entirely in your browser.
